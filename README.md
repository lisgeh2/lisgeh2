### Hi, I'm Lisa 👋

I study **Psychology at the University of Zurich** with a minor in **Computer Science**,
and I like building the tools I wish existed – questionnaires, models, dashboards,
little automations. Most of what you see here started as *"wouldn't it be cool if…"*.

I'm looking to bring that into **research projects**: programming experiments and
online studies, cleaning and analysing data, and building tools that make a lab's
everyday work easier.

#### 🔬 Selected projects

| | |
|---|---|
| 🧠 **[Neural Category Lab](https://github.com/lisgeh2/computational_modeling)** | Interactive neural-network model of human category learning – watch it learn, inspect single neurons, "lesion" them. Plain JavaScript, with tests. |
| 📝 **[Questionnaire template](https://github.com/lisgeh2/questionnaire_template)** | My own engine for multi-page online questionnaires: define items as data, get validation, storage, scoring, personal feedback and a results dashboard. |
| 🌶️ **[How Neurospicy Are You?](https://github.com/lisgeh2/how-neurospicy-are-you)** | A self-assessment built on that template – scores ADHD and autism traits and shows each person where they fall relative to clinical and norm groups. |
| 🤝 **[Notion Friends CRM](https://github.com/lisgeh2/notion_api)** | Reads my calendar, logs who I met in Notion and reminds me when to reach out again. Runs on its own via GitHub Actions. |
| 🚆 **[SBB Display](https://github.com/lisgeh2/SBB-display)** | Live public-transport departures on a Raspberry Pi OLED screen. |
| 🎭 **[Impro-Abende Zürich](https://github.com/lisgeh2/stuthe-website)** | Website for the student improv theatre evenings at UZH & ETH. |
